RizeAbove DataWorks — About Us

RizeAbove DataWorks was created from a simple truth: when you’re deep into a game you love, the right information at the right moment makes the experience better. Planet Crafter Companion began as a personal tool — something built because the data I needed wasn’t easily accessible while playing. I wanted clarity, speed, and accuracy without fumbling through menus or breaking immersion. So I built the tool I wished existed.

Companion was developed from the inside out. The database came first, designed to be structured, reliable, and easy to maintain. The interface grew naturally from that foundation, shaped by real gameplay needs and the desire for a clean, frictionless experience. If a feature didn’t help in the moment, it didn’t make the cut.

To keep the app current, I built automated scripts that extract game data directly from its source and convert it into the exact format the app requires. This ensures Companion stays accurate through updates and expansions — not through guesswork, but through repeatable, dependable processes.

RizeAbove DataWorks may be a one-person studio, but the mission is bigger: create tools that feel professional, polished, and genuinely useful to players. I use Planet Crafter Companion every time I play, and I’m committed to keeping it updated, refined, and ready for anyone who finds value in it.

Built by a player. Designed for players. Powered by data, shaped by experience.
