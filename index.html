About RizeAbove DataWorks

RizeAbove DataWorks was founded by Gregory Lee Nason, a maker at heart who believes technology should feel simple, helpful, and built with genuine care. What started as a handful of personal projects — a companion app for a favorite game, a dice game inspired by family traditions, and a recipe app created to preserve the meals his mother loves — grew into a mission to build software that rises above the ordinary — crafting tools that make life easier, games that bring joy, and everyday helpers built with intention, empathy, and a commitment to clarity.

Founder & Creator — Gregory Lee Nason

With over 30 years of experience in IT support, operations, and technical problem solving, Greg shifted from supporting systems to creating them. A lifelong learner and hands-on builder, Greg founded RizeAbove DataWorks to build software with heart — bringing a rare blend of experience, craftsmanship, and humanity to every project.
