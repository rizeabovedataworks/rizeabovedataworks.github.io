The Story Behind the Game

I learned the game of 10,000 ten years ago, and it quickly became one of our family and friends' absolute favorite tabletop games. We’ve shared so many unforgettable nights gathered around the dining table, rolling dice, laughing, and competing. I wanted to build an app that served two real-world needs: let you play a game of 10,000 anytime against smart AI, AND serve as a live digital scorekeeper when friends and family come over to play around the table. After 3 complete rebuilds and a month of passionate crafting, 10,000 Dice & Score Keeper was born!

Dual-Mode Game Features
