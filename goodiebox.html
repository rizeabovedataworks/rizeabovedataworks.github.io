The Goodie Box

Preserving Mom's Kitchen & the Meals We Grew Up Loving

A digital family heirloom recipe chest designed to preserve cherished home recipes, family kitchen memories, and baking traditions across generations.

The Story Behind the Recipe Chest

My mom is 91 years young as of this writing, and I wanted to preserve all those beloved home-cooked recipes I grew up loving. Nothing too fancy, nothing too bold — but I always remember fresh cookies sitting in the cookie jar, warm family dinners on the table, and homemade birthday cakes, all coming straight from Mom and her kitchen.

I created The Goodie Box to share Mom's cherished recipes, allow families to easily add and organize their own treasured meals, and deliver new family recipes Over-The-Air over time.

❤️ Memories Spotlight

Real stories captured inside The Goodie Box.

From "Mom's Best Classic Peanut Brittle":

"She'd take the cookie sheet out to the garage and set it on a towel on the hood of the car to cool. The frigid garage temps usually in the single digits chilled the peanut brittles foam in a few minutes but Mom insisted it stay out there for an hour or so. Then she'd bring it in and we'd crack it together. She'd always let me steal a few pieces."

From "Mom's Chocolate Chip Cookies":

"One of the most popular cookie jars was the elf jar... What I remember was that it was NEVER without cookies. Usually chocolate chip ones. Whenever it got low Mom would make a new batch... My house had some of the most wonderful smells coming out of it. I owe it all to Mom's amazing baking and cooking skills."
