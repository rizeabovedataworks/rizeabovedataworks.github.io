Privacy Policy & Data Security

100% Local Storage. Zero Data Collection. Zero Tracking.

Your privacy and data security are fundamental. This policy applies to all applications published by RizeAbove DataWorks.

Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy and Data Security Disclosure applies to all mobile applications developed and published by RizeAbove DataWorks (Greg Nason), including:

  • Planet Crafter Companion (Android App)
  • 10,000 Dice Keeper (Android App)
  • The Goodie Box (Android App)

Core Privacy & Data Security Commitments

🛡️

Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, track, harvest, sell, or share any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifier).

💾

100% Local Device Storage

All app data — including saved base plans, game checklists, 10k dice game scores, custom family recipes, and settings — is saved 100% locally in your device's private app sandbox.

🚫

No Intrusive Ads or Trackers

Our applications contain zero third-party ad networks, zero analytics tracking SDKs, zero behavioral profiling, and zero user data monetization.

📡

Network Usage Disclosure

Planet Crafter Companion connects over secure HTTPS solely to query public GitHub datasets for over-the-air game updates. No user data is transmitted during update checks.

🔐

Android Data Security

Because your data remains 100% local on your device, it is protected by standard Android operating system sandbox security and hardware encryption protocols.

👶

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13 years of age, complying fully with COPPA guidelines.

External Links & Third-Party Services

If you choose to click external links within our apps or website (such as Google Play Store, PayPal developer tip jar, or Discord community links), you will be directed to external third-party websites operating under their own respective privacy policies. RizeAbove DataWorks does not control third-party websites or payment processors.
