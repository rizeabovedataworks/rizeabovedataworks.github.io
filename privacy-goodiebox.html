Privacy Policy

The Goodie Box — Digital Family Heirloom Recipe Chest

Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Publisher: RizeAbove DataWorks LLC

Summary Notice: The Goodie Box is designed with 100% user data privacy at its core. We do not collect, track, sell, or share any personal information, usage metrics, or user-generated recipe content.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe your personal family memories and heirloom recipes belong entirely to you. The Goodie Box does NOT collect any personal identifying information, including:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising IDs
  • User usage analytics, telemetries, or behavioral tracking

2. Local Device Data Storage

All user-generated content—including custom family recipes, personal kitchen notes, dish photos, and favorites—is stored 100% locally on your device in an encrypted local database. Your custom recipes and photos remain on your phone and are never uploaded to third-party servers.

3. Network Communications & Cloud Updates

The application connects to the Internet solely to perform Over-The-Air (OTA) recipe dataset updates from the official public repository on GitHub (rizeabovedataworks.github.io). All network communications are transmitted using secure HTTPS encryption. No user data is sent during cloud sync operations.

4. Device Permissions

The Goodie Box requests only minimal, necessary device permissions:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly to check for and download official OTA recipe updates.
  • Photo/Media Picker (PickVisualMedia): Used solely when you manually choose to attach a photo from your gallery to a custom recipe. The app does not access any other photos on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all custom recipes and notes are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can edit or delete your custom recipes inside the application at any time, or permanently clear all local data by uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage in your Android settings.
