Preserving the Meals We Grew Up Loving

A heartfelt family recipe preserver sharing Mom's cherished recipes and protecting your family's culinary heritage across generations.

The Story Behind the App

My mom is 91 years young as of this writing, and I wanted to preserve all those beloved home-cooked recipes I grew up loving. Nothing too fancy, nothing too bold — but I always remember fresh cookies sitting in the cookie jar, warm family dinners on the table, and homemade birthday cakes, all coming straight from Mom and her kitchen. I created Mom's Kitchen to share Mom's cherished recipes, allow families to easily add and organize their own treasured meals, and deliver new family recipes Over-The-Air over time.
